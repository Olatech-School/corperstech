# CorpersTech Knowledge Base Report

## 1. 16 Operational Category Structure

The CorpersTech Knowledge Center supports exactly 16 critical operational categories designed to partition information logically across all Olatech administrative staff divisions:

1. **Getting Started**: Onboarding guidelines and credentials setup manuals for new staff members.
2. **Admissions Operations**: SOPs for review procedures, reference code structures, and communication dispatches.
3. **Career Officer Handbook**: Guidelines for placement sourcing, cv audits, and interview preparations.
4. **Recruitment AI Manual**: Step-by-step procedures for auditing and publishing AI-scraped career opportunities.
5. **Transportation Operations**: Bus routes coordination, terminal schedules, and baggage controls.
6. **Support Operations**: SLA ticket resolution guides, troubleshooting, and student helpdesk tools.
7. **Finance Operations**: Installment verification, sponsorship tracking, and bank draft reconciliation.
8. **Staff Administration**: Internal operational policies, leave coordination, and timesheets.
9. **Command Center User Guide**: Navigation tips and visual dashboard explanations.
10. **Reporting & Analytics**: Guidelines for exporting CSV files and generating executive reports.
11. **Security Policies**: Passcode overriding, session timeouts, and privacy compliance rules.
12. **System Maintenance**: Database backups, manual pruning, and server updates.
13. **Emergency Procedures**: Server outages, emergency contacts, and student safety.
14. **Troubleshooting**: Command center frozen sessions and system load failures.
15. **Frequently Asked Questions**: Direct answers to common operational inquiries.
16. **Release Notes**: Versions log tracking structural software updates on CorpersTech.

## 2. Interactive Guides Layout Standards

To maintain a professional developer-portal feel, every manual inside the database utilizes a structured, human-readable section standard:

- **Estimated Reading Time**: Calculated dynamically on content length at roughly 150 words-per-minute.
- **Difficulty Index**: Easy, Medium, or Advanced depending on overall manual length.
- **Overview & Objectives**: Explains what the guide accomplishes and the high-level business why.
- **Step-by-Step Instructions**: Enumerated clicks and exact terminal/system inputs.
- **Tips & Warnings**: Visual admonition blocks containing critical procedural callouts.
- **Best Practices**: Performance hacks for active staff.
- **Common Mistakes**: Frequent traps that degrade admissions or career performance.
