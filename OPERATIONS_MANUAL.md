# CorpersTech v1.0 — Operations Manual

This manual provides instructions for administrators of the **Olatech School of Programming** using the Admissions & Operations CRM platform.

---

## 1. Admissions CRM Workflow

To manage incoming candidate applications, select the **Admissions Command Center** tab in your main header.

### Step 1: Review Queue
- Check the dashboard metrics for new registrations count.
- Scroll down to the **Review Queue (Pending Applications)**.
- Select **Details** to review the candidate's personal details, NYSC documents status, and selected courses.
- Choose **Approve** or **Reject** to transition the applicant's record.

### Step 2: Cohort Mapping
- Open the **Learning Cohorts** workspace.
- Create new batches (e.g. `Batch A Stream I Cybersecurity`) with assigned trainers and expected start dates.
- Navigate to the applicant list to assign individual candidates into their matching training cohorts.

### Step 3: Logistics & Transportation
- In the **Commute Manager** tab, view candidates who selected **Company Bus** for transport.
- Allocate pickup station options (e.g. `Lagos Mainland`, `Lagos Island`) and view pickup coordinators details.
- Use the **Print Passenger Manifest** button to generate hardcopy logs for bus drivers.

---

## 2. Orientation Checklist Guidelines

Every candidate must satisfy 6 fundamental checks before being marked fully active for classes:
1. **Documents verified**: NYSC Call-up Letter and green card submission confirmed.
2. **Laptop confirmed**: Verification that the applicant has a development computer (8GB+ RAM).
3. **Payment confirmed**: Registration fee payment verified.
4. **Orientation attended**: Completion of the physical or streaming induction.
5. **WhatsApp group joined**: Addition to the course communication forum.
6. **Ready for class**: Overall checklist score indicates `100%`.

---

## 3. Exporting Admissions Data
- Click **Export CSV** inside the applicant's directory to download a fully detailed spreadsheet containing the current filtered list.
- Use the standard browser print hotkey (`Ctrl + P` / `Cmd + P`) on reports layouts to save high-contrast PDF documents for administrative archives.
