# CorpersTech Staff Productivity Report

## 1. Personal Workspace Efficiency

Every staff member is equipped with a **Personal Productivity** console on their Operations Center dashboard.

### Metrics Computed:
- **Task List Clearance**: Percentage of overall daily tasks successfully marked as completed.
- **SLA Responsiveness**: Average triage response time (1.8 hours) for answering customer enquiries.
- **Cheklists Pending**: Count of uncompleted deliverables for the active shift.

## 2. Shift Notepad & Reminders

A custom notepad enables the creation of temporary reminders and task lists. These are stored securely under their email configuration profile, allowing staff members to resume work seamlessly across any browser session.
